SOP-01: Tissue Fixation Protocol

Immerse organ biopsies in 10% Neutral Buffered Formalin (NBF) at a minimum 1:20 tissue-to-fixative volume ratio. Maintain fixation at room temperature (20–25°C) for 24 to 48 hours prior to paraffin embedding.

ac_unit

SOP-02: -80°C Cryopreservation

For genomic and RNA-seq integrity, snap-freeze biopsies in liquid nitrogen-cooled isopentane within 15 minutes of collection. Transfer immediately to pre-chilled cryo-tubes and archive in -80°C Freezers (Sector S1/S2).

palette

SOP-03: Multi-Stain Histology Triplet

Serial 4 µm microtome sections are stained in triplicate: (1) Hematoxylin & Eosin (H&E) for baseline morphology; (2) Masson's Trichrome for interstitial fibrosis (IFTA); (3) Periodic Acid-Schiff (PAS) for glomerulosclerosis mapping.

warning

SOP-04: Prolonged Ischemia Directives

Cold ischemia times exceeding 4 hours trigger automatic flag status. Mandatory correlative photoacoustic and ULM velocity profiling must be performed to assess cortico-medullary junction microvascular patency.
